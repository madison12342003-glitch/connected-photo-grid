# Color System

## Default Western Sichuan editorial palette
Use warm white or off-white as the main background, black/charcoal for line art and type, and restrained red as the graphic accent. Let the original photographs supply natural greens, earth browns, snow whites, sky blues, and prayer-flag colors.

Suggested starting palette:
- warm white: #F7F5F0
- paper cream: #EEE9DF
- charcoal: #242321
- ink black: #111111
- muted vermilion: #B52E32
- pale neutral grey: #D8D5CF

These are starting values, not rigid requirements.

## Distribution
- Backgrounds: mostly warm white/off-white.
- Text and line art: black or charcoal.
- Red: small but repeated accents that help the nine tiles cohere.
- Photographic colors: retain naturally, with only gentle global correction when necessary.

## Rules
- Match overall contrast and temperature, not every photograph's exact hue.
- Do not recolor snow, grass, sky, or buildings merely to fit a palette.
- Use red in several controlled places rather than one oversized unrelated block.
- Test the collage against a white social-media interface; avoid a dark outer field if it creates an abrupt border.
- Keep the accent color count small so natural photographs remain dominant.

## Review
Check the full collage in color and grayscale. The composition should still have clear hierarchy without depending on saturation alone.
