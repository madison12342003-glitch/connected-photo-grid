# Western Sichuan Multi-Layer Grid — Example Plan

## Project intent
Create a nine-tile editorial collage for a white-background social feed. Preserve the documentary feel of the user's Western Sichuan photographs while adding a restrained off-white, black, and red graphic system.

## Candidate material visible in the collection
Possible subjects include snowy ridges, mountain roads, forested slopes, broad plateau valleys, prayer flags, a town marker, Tibetan Buddhist architecture, and pink flowers. Final photo selection must be made from the actual accessible source files; this example is a planning guide, not a claim that every attachment has been re-audited.

## Visual direction
- Background: warm white/off-white.
- Type and line art: black/charcoal.
- Accent: restrained vermilion red.
- Photography: keep natural color, with gentle consistency correction only.
- Illustration: simple black line art or restrained red print style.
- Text: real editable text, checked manually; never rasterized pseudo-Tibetan script.

## Nine-tile role map
A. Opening tile: strongest scenic photograph plus a small title or line-art detail.
B. Landscape bridge: mountain or forest image; a ridge/contour can echo into C.
C. Graphic pause: off-white field, fine mountain line art, short verified text.
D. Cultural tile: prayer flags or a real architectural photograph with minimal framing.
E. Center anchor: the most iconic landscape or town-view photograph; keep uncluttered.
F. Detail tile: a sign, prayer-flag detail, or texture crop with restrained red accent.
G. Botanical tile: flower photograph or a flower detail with line-art echo.
H. Architecture tile: temple/building photograph with careful respect for the subject.
I. Closing tile: road, valley, or mountain image that resolves the visual movement.

This map is a starting hypothesis. Rearrange roles after comparing the actual photographs and their edge features.

## Motif system
Use only a few repeated motifs: mountain contour, prayer-flag color sequence, flower stem, and a red mark or line. Do not place every motif in every tile. Avoid adding yaks unless the source material or user's direction supports their inclusion.

## Acceptance checklist
- Nine tiles read as one editorial work.
- Each tile has a clear focal point.
- Background remains light enough for white social-feed surroundings.
- Natural photo colors are not flattened by the graphic palette.
- Text is accurate and legible.
- Any cross-tile line is aligned on the full canvas before slicing.
- No generated element falsely claims to be documentary evidence.
